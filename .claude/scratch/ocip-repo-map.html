  2. P1 · structural Document or recreate AllRoles_clean.csv — nine notebooks in the historical pipeline depend on it directly or indirectly, and none of them produce it. Right now this repo can't rebuild its own most-used dataset from a clean checkout.
  3. P1 · structural Extract the QCEW helpers into tools/qcew.py — the ~25-line QCEW_SECTOR_TITLES dict and naics_to_qcew_sector() are hand-copied across at least six cells in three different notebooks. Constants like MAX_DESCRIPTION_CHARS have already drifted between copies (1200 → 2000 → 3000) — a shared module removes that risk going forward.
  4. P2 · consolidation Trim public_data_fetch/public.ipynb to its cells 0–1 (the actual QCEW fetch). Cells 2–5 are dead weight — superseded by placements_analysis.ipynb per that notebook's own docstring.
  5. P2 · consolidation Fold validate_application_data.ipynb into ccl26_matchoutcomes.ipynb — the scoping/dedup logic is duplicated between them; keep one.
  6. P2 · consolidation Confirm whether quantumqueryMN.ipynb is still needed — its only input no longer exists in data/. If its "Program at a Glance" numbers are already superseded by ccl26_matchoutcomes.ipynb, archive it.
  7. P3 · naming Rename ccl25_match_outcomes.ipynb to reflect that it's Spring Forward 2026, not a 2025 cycle — e.g. sf26_match_outcomes.ipynb.
  8. P3 · naming Rename placement_join/bruh.ipynb to something that describes what it does — it's the hub-composition sanity check that gates naming a deck Finding after a QCEW sector.
  9. P3 · hygiene Reconcile the Cultural Corps "Step N of 5" numbering — Steps 1–3 live in culturalcorps.ipynb, Step 4 in culturalcorpsmerge.ipynb, and no Step 5 exists anywhere.
  10. P3 · hygiene Give the *_BACKUP_before_*.csv files a home — every merge/weight script writes one before mutating a shared pipeline file. Good instinct, but nothing prunes them; a data/_backups/ convention would keep them from accumulating silently.
